# About

**InContext** is a working reference on the craft of context for AI.

The premise: large models don't fail because they're stupid. They fail because they're working with the wrong context — too little, too much, the wrong shape, the wrong order, or the right material buried under noise. The skill of getting useful work out of an AI is, increasingly, the skill of curating what it sees.

This site is a place to write that down. Not a blog, not a book — a reference. Pages stand alone, get edited as understanding sharpens, and link sideways to whatever's related.

Built with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and the [Just the Docs](https://just-the-docs.com/) theme. Source on [GitHub](https://github.com/YOUR-USER/incontext) — corrections and additions welcome.
